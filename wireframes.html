Vibe

Instagram drop meets luxury brand. Energy that teens recognize instantly, but editorial enough for parents. The hot pink reads bold without being garish on a cream background.

Motion

Tiles stagger in as you scroll (0.05s delay per tile). Hover scales to 1.02. Lightbox cross-fades between images (300ms opacity swap). All CSS, zero JS overhead.

Tradeoff

Masonry grids can clip tall portrait images awkwardly. We'd set column heights intentionally. The hot pink accent may feel loud in two years — but it's on-brand for a 16th birthday now.

Vibe

Apple Photos meets Instagram Stories. The hero image cycles (Ken Burns pan/zoom). The filmstrip lets you scrub through favorites fast. Teens will immediately recognize the pattern.

Motion

Hero: slow 8s pan + scale (CSS keyframe, looping). Dot indicator: slides between positions on tap. Filmstrip: CSS scroll-snap. Lightbox: directional slide-in (left goes left, right goes right).

Tradeoff

Most complex to build of the three. The auto-cycling hero needs one featured photo chosen intentionally — ideally you pick it, not randomized. Filmstrip on desktop feels slightly foreign; it's native on mobile.

Vibe

High-end photography portfolio meets a private album drop. The feature layout makes it feel curated, not just dumped. Mothers will love the editorial quality. Teens will share individual photos from the lightbox.

Motion

Scroll: tiles fade up with stagger (IntersectionObserver, 60ms delay per row). Hover: scale 1.04 with shadow lift. Lightbox: slides left or right depending on direction, then snaps. All at 60fps on iPhone.

Tradeoff

Closest to current design — lowest rebuild cost. The feature section requires you to pick a hero shot. Sage is more timeless than hot pink, but less energetic. Best choice if this template will be reused for future shoots.
